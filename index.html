The problem
A structural gap

Below roughly €20 million the bank balance sheet still works; above ~€250 million the public bond market starts. Between them, proven mid-cap credit has no efficient market to clear in — every tradeable deal is hand-built, and the fixed cost kills the ticket.

Why now
Five forces

Basel rules push banks out of exactly this size; private credit and Schuldschein hit their limits; the Nordic precedent runs at scale; AI collapses the preparation cost; and one tested standard now exists for everyone. None of these existed together five years ago.

€800bnper year in additional investment the Draghi report says Europe needs — a political mandate that proves urgency, never a solution

One standard

Senior secured, trustee-governed floating-rate notes — 3m EURIBOR + 450–975, 3–4 year tenor, covenants from a fixed menu, €100,000 denominations, listed and transferable from day one. Six to eight weeks from mandate to settlement.

VanillaBonds is the working name of a company in formation (Munich, Germany). It is not authorised under the WpIG or the KWG and conducts no activity requiring such authorisation; this website is informational only. Under the intended architecture, investor onboarding, suitability and placement are performed by a licensed investment firm under whose licence VanillaBonds would act as a tied agent (vertraglich gebundener Vermittler); that architecture is subject to legal confirmation. VanillaBonds is never the issuer, never a principal and never a lender: it does not lend, does not hold bonds or client money, and issues no credit ratings — the issuer fact sheet delivers sourced facts and comparability and is not a credit rating within the meaning of Regulation (EC) 1060/2009. Nothing on this site constitutes an offer of securities, a solicitation of an offer, investment advice or an investment recommendation. Any future offering would be addressed exclusively to qualified/professional investors within the meaning of MiFID II, with minimum denominations of €100,000, in reliance on applicable prospectus exemptions, and would be arranged together with an appropriately licensed partner firm. Market figures are drawn from public sources (incl. the 2024 Draghi report and Nordic market statistics) and our own issuer dataset; they describe the market, not an investment proposition.
