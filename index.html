Why not today

Four reasons it does not happen. Vanilla's answer to each.

TodayWith Vanilla

Bespoke work

  • Every financing built from scratch, per lender.
  • Costs as much at €30m as at €300m.

One standard bond

  • Common terms, one format.
  • No new design per deal.

Finding the companies

  • Funds lend to companies a private-equity owner brings.
  • A family company has nobody who does that.

Sourcing with a first check

  • Via advisers, CFOs, banks and a portal.
  • No adviser is replaced.

A fund sorts instead of decides

  • Many proposals, few that fit, all in different forms.
  • Companies apply to funds that never fit.

Pre-qualified proposals

  • On the fund's own criteria, in its format.
  • Ready to decide on. Both sides save time.

No first decision

  • Nobody commits first or sets the terms.
  • For €30m from funds, nobody arranges it. The deal never starts.

Anchors go first

  • Two or three funds reserve capacity upfront.
  • In return: first look, the price, most of each bond.

How it runs

Anchors first. Ten weeks, every bond.

  1. SourcingDays
    • Via adviser, bank or directly.
    • First check: €50–500m revenue, three audited years, debt ≤ 3.5× EBITDA, interest cover ≥ 2.5×.
  2. Fact fileWeeks 1–4
    • The company's plan in the anchors' format, every fact with its source.
    • The CFO confirms. Capital structure settled before launch.
  3. Anchor decisionWeeks 5–6
    • Capacity reserved upfront.
    • Binding ticket in ten working days.
  4. One standard bondWeeks 7–10
    • Co-investors join on the same terms.
    • Issued only when fully covered.
  5. After closingYears 1–5
    • Nordic Trustee tests the covenants each quarter.
    • The company reports quarterly and repays at maturity.

One deal, illustrative: how a €30 million bond is filled

  • Three anchors · €7m each · 70%
  • Four co-investors · €9m · 30%
  • Five years, Euribor + 600bp.
  • Anchors commit before the mandate; co-investors take the rest.
  • €690,000 (2.3%) paid once by the company, at closing.
