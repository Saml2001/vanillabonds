The problem

Europe’s funding map has a hole in the middle

Three channels serve European corporate debt — and each one stops short of the ordinary mid-cap.

01 — BANK SYNDICATES

Don’t clear below ~€75–100m

Syndicate desks carry a cost base that doesn’t work on small tickets. Below the threshold, issuers are left with club loans on bank terms — slow, covenant-heavy, and capital-constrained as banks retreat from mid-cap risk.

02 — SCHULDSCHEIN

A conservative pool that just lost trust

The 2024–26 restructurings (Varta, BayWa, KTM) hit a generalist investor base with no trustee, no collective action clauses and no exit. Unrated, smaller and non-German names are being shown the door.

03 — SYNDICATED HIGH YIELD

Starts near €200m

Rated, prospectus-based benchmark issuance is built for large caps. The documentation, rating and marketing cost simply doesn’t amortise on a €30m deal.

In between sits the ordinary, healthy European mid-cap — with nowhere to issue. The €20–75m band is where we operate, on a format that already works: over 90% of German SME high yield has quietly moved to Nordic documentation.
The platform

One standard. One process. One investor circle.

One standard

Nordic bond terms: senior secured, trustee-governed, majority decisions through collective action clauses, a fixed covenant menu. Documentation is standardised, so counsel reviews the delta — not two hundred pages from scratch.

One process

Mandate to settlement in six to eight weeks. No prospectus and no mandatory rating in the professional segment (€100,000 minimum denominations). ISIN, Euroclear/Clearstream settlement and paying agent through established providers — tradeable from day one.

One investor circle

Professional and institutional investors onboard once, then see standardised, comparable deal flow matched to their domain — instead of repeating per-deal paperwork for every single ticket.
